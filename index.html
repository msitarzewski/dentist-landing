Go ahead, ask.

What does a first visit cost without insurance?

$195 at Feast Dental in East Austin, and it takes 75 minutes. That covers a full exam, X-rays, a regular cleaning, and a written plan with prices. If your gums need more than a regular cleaning, the fee is higher, and I show you why and give you the price in writing first.

Do you take my insurance?

Add a photo of your card when you book. We’ll check your plan and tell you whether we’re in-network, and what out-of-network would cost, before you come. What we tell you is an estimate from your plan, not a promise from your insurer. You still pay your plan’s deductible and copay.

I haven’t been in years. Is that a problem?

No. I’ll ask when your last visit was, and there’s no wrong answer. I went four years without one myself.

What if I’m scared of the dentist?

Say so when you walk in. You set the pace from there, and you can text us a question before you book.

Will it hurt?

Sometimes, a little. The numbing is a pinch, and you can be sore afterward. I numb the area first and check it’s working before I start. I tell you what’s next before I do it. Raise a hand and I stop.

How long is the first visit?

75 minutes. That’s the exam and X-rays, a regular cleaning if your gums are ready for one, and time to go over the plan.

Does whitening hurt?

It can make teeth sensitive, usually for a few days. I check yours first and tell you what to expect. You can stop any time.

What does a filling cost without insurance?

From $190 for a small tooth-colored filling. Bigger fillings and back teeth cost more. I draw the spot and give you the price in writing before I start.

What does an emergency visit cost?

$95 covers the look and an X-ray of that tooth. The fix is extra, and I price it before I start. After hours, call or text the same number. If your face is swelling or it’s hard to breathe or swallow, call 911.

Are you open evenings or Saturdays?

Yes. Tuesdays and Thursdays till 7:30 pm, and Saturdays from 9 am to 1 pm.

What if I need to cancel?

Call or text as soon as you know and we’ll find you another time. Cancel by 5 pm the day before and there’s no fee.
