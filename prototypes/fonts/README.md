# Test fonts (prototype only)

Three variable display faces used by `../bite.html` section E to measure axis animation. They are not the
chosen brand fonts and are not referenced by the site; `assets/fonts/` stays empty until the face is picked.

| File | Family | Axes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `anybody-latin-wdth-wght.woff2` | Anybody | wdth 50-150, wght 100-900 | Google Fonts, `fonts.gstatic.com/s/anybody/v13` (latin subset) |
| `archivo-latin-wdth-wght.woff2` | Archivo | wdth 62-125, wght 100-900 | Google Fonts, `fonts.gstatic.com/s/archivo/v25` (latin subset) |
| `saira-latin-wdth-wght.woff2` | Saira | wdth 50-125, wght 100-900 | Google Fonts, `fonts.gstatic.com/s/saira/v23` (latin subset) |

Downloaded 2026-11-09. All three are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the licence texts are the
`OFL-*.txt` files beside them (from the `google/fonts` repository).
