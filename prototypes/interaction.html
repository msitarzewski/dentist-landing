1. The bite on press

Press: the pill squashes 4% toward its bottom-left. Click: the corner is gone in one frame, crumbs leave, the pill springs back. It rests bitten for 2 seconds or until it leaves the screen, then restocks. A second click replays the spring and removes nothing more.
